"Bag for Life" campaign for Harrods

brand identity, marketing campaign, packaging

"Bag For Life" campaign proposal for Harrods

Brief: To go further in its desire of more eco-awareness and sustainability, Harrods is now launching the Bag for Life campaign.

Non-woven polypropylene bag. Non-toxic, reusable and recyclable material. Water-resistant. Size suitable for any shopping item including shoe boxes. Technique used: Heat transfer printing for vibrant colours and crips lines.

Limited editions in partnership with artists specialised in collages inspired by the fashion world. Creating art with existing materials can be considered as a form of recycling.

Limited edition branded reusable, washable coffee cups.

Emailing announcing the launch of the Bag for Life campaign.

Campaign awareness in-situ. Animated plasma screens.

about me

Hey! I'm Emma, a freelance graphic designer with 8 years of extensive experience in the vast field of graphic design. I’ve navigated through a wide range of exciting projects, offering creative solutions for both large companies abroad and small local associations.

My expertise covers brand identity, design of print and digital communication tools, photo retouching, photography and more.

I have a sharp eye for detail and a meticulous approach to creating designs that are not only consistent with the target audience and the company’s values, but also carefully crafted and refreshing.
